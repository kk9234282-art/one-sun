Open all-India ZIP (CSV files)

Select your ZIP directly from this computer. CSVs are streamed and stored in the browser’s local database; the archive is not uploaded or unpacked into RAM all at once. This mode uses the ZIP contents as a separate dataset, not a sum of state files plus the published subset. State/NIC/financial information is never guessed.
